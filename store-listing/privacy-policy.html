Privacy Policy

FlowSense – AI Expense Tracker  |  Last updated: April 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FlowSense – AI Expense Tracker ("FlowSense", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our Android application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Privacy-first design: FlowSense is built on a local-first principle. All financial data, SMS content, location metadata, and receipt images are stored exclusively on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers. Data is only transmitted to third-party AI services when you explicitly opt in to Cloud AI features.

1. Information We Collect

FlowSense collects information only to provide and improve the App's features. All information is stored locally on your device unless stated otherwise.

1.1 Financial Transaction Data

When you log an expense or income — manually, by scanning a receipt, via voice input, or through automatic SMS/notification capture — the App stores:

  • Transaction amount and currency
  • Merchant or payee name
  • Category, tags, and notes you assign
  • Date and time of the transaction
  • Transaction source (manual, OCR scan, SMS, notification, voice, import)
  • Split-expense details if you record a shared payment

All financial records are stored in a JSON file inside the App's private data directory (/data/data/com.flowsense.app/), which is inaccessible to other apps.

1.2 Camera and Receipt Images

When you use the receipt scanner, the App activates your device camera and captures a photo. The image is:

  • Processed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit OCR and/or Tesseract
  • Stored locally in the App's private storage as a URI reference
  • Never uploaded to any server

1.3 SMS Messages

If you grant the Read SMS / Receive SMS permission, FlowSense reads incoming SMS messages to detect banking transactions. This processing is:

  • Performed entirely on your device
  • Limited to messages that match known banking sender patterns
  • Never transmitted to FlowSense servers or any third party

You can revoke SMS permission at any time in Android Settings without losing existing data.

1.4 Notification Content

If you enable the Notification Listener service, FlowSense reads notifications from your banking and payment apps (e.g., Chase, PayPal, Venmo) to detect transactions automatically. Notification content is:

  • Processed on-device only
  • Stored solely as parsed transaction records
  • Never transmitted externally

1.5 Location Data

If you grant location permission, FlowSense records GPS coordinates at the time of a transaction to create a merchant location pin on the Store Map. This data:

  • Is stored locally on your device as transaction metadata
  • Is used only within the App (map view, location-based grouping)
  • Is never transmitted to FlowSense or any third party
  • Background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) is requested solely to geo-tag transactions that are auto-captured from SMS while the App is not in the foreground

1.6 Audio / Voice Input

If you use the voice transaction entry feature, the App activates your microphone to capture spoken input. Audio is:

  • Processed locally to extract transaction details
  • Not recorded, saved as audio files, or transmitted anywhere

1.7 Installed Applications

The App uses the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission to detect which banking or payment apps are installed on your device. This is used exclusively to configure the notification listener to monitor the correct apps. This information:

  • Is never stored as a persistent list
  • Is never transmitted to any server

1.8 AI Conversation History

When you interact with the AI assistant inside the App, your prompts and the AI's responses are stored locally on your device as conversation history. This history is used only to provide context in future AI queries within the same session.

1.9 App Settings and API Keys

Your preferences (currency, AI mode, notification settings, budget limits) and any third-party API keys you enter (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek) are stored locally on your device. API keys are not transmitted to FlowSense. They are sent only to the respective AI service provider when you initiate a Cloud AI query.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by the App is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your expense dashboard, reports, and budget summaries
  • Generating AI-powered spending insights (on-device or via Cloud AI)
  • Geo-tagging transactions and displaying the Store Map
  • Detecting and parsing banking transactions from SMS and notifications
  • Sending local budget-overspend alerts and reminders (no external servers)
  • Enabling export / import of your data (CSV or JSON)

5. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored in the App's private data directory on your device, which Android protects from access by other applications. Data is not stored on FlowSense servers.

  • Storage format: Structured JSON file in app-private storage
  • Backup: Android auto-backup may include app data per your device backup settings. You can exclude FlowSense from Android backup in your device settings if you prefer.
  • Transit encryption: All network requests (exchange rate lookups and Cloud AI queries) use HTTPS/TLS.
  • API keys: Stored in the app's private storage; never transmitted to FlowSense.

6. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data within the App:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App's Dashboard, Transactions, and Reports screens.
  • Export: Go to Settings → Export / Import to export all data as a JSON or CSV file.
  • Delete all data: Go to Settings → Export / Import → Delete All Data to permanently erase all stored information from your device.
  • Revoke permissions: You may revoke any permission (SMS, camera, location, microphone) at any time in Android Settings → Apps → FlowSense → Permissions. Revoking a permission disables the related feature but does not delete previously captured data.
  • Disable notification listener: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Special App Access → Notification Access and disable FlowSense.

7. Children's Privacy

FlowSense is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is rated Everyone on Google Play and does not contain content designed for children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and stored personal information, please contact us and we will help you remove it from the device.

8. Analytics and Advertising

FlowSense contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs. We do not track your in-app behavior, display ads, or share usage data with advertisers or analytics platforms.

9. In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. FlowSense does not collect or store your payment card details. Purchase history is managed by Google. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle billing data.
